# CurriLoop v9.0.41 Retry Tail Drain Audit

## Scope
- Preserve v9.0.40 miss-event retry persistence.
- Remove end-of-round tail starvation for the last 3–4 misses.
- Do not alter curriculum source data or presentation granularity.

## Invariants
1. A miss schedules a retry independently of later correction in the main field.
2. During normal progression, retry delay remains 3 answer attempts.
3. At the final task of the current round/scope, all remaining retry records for that round/scope are promoted to due immediately.
4. `다음` does not advance to a new round while such retry records remain.
5. At the round tail, retry failure or `나중에` remains due without waiting for nonexistent future main answers.
6. Retry-card success, retry OFF, and review reset remain the supported removal paths.
